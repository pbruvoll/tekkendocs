== MD (''Mandinga'') ==

Mandinga is a unique install that Eddy gains upon landing certain moves on hit or upon activating Heat. Eddy begins at Mandinga level zero, and each hit increases this level by one, up to a maximum of two. Gaining one or two Mandinga levels allows Eddy to use the base or powered up versions respectively of Yansan Mandinga (MD.HSP.3+4) and Ogum Mandinga (MD.HSP.b+4). At level one, Eddy's Bananeira stance gains an automatic low parry property that transitions into Ogum Mandinga. Unlike other characters' installs, Eddy's Mandinga level is ''not'' reset after the usage of a Mandinga-only move, and remains until the end of the round.
