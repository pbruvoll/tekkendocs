# Arabic Tekken glossary

Terminology for translating tekkendocs character guides into Arabic (`ar`, Modern Standard Arabic).
**Append to this file** whenever a run needs a term that is not here, and record where it came from.

## Approach

Written Arabic Tekken material is thin: Arabic gaming press (Saudi Gamer, Arab Showtime, Arabic
Wikipedia) and short videos. The press writes the general fighting vocabulary in Arabic (ضربة، صد،
رمية، الخصم، كومبو، فريمات، جولة، ضرر، درع) and keeps the competitive jargon and system names in
English, often in Latin script inside the Arabic sentence (Heat, Heat Smash, Power Crush, Hit
Confirm, Whiff punish, Frame trap). Follow that split, but write the English jargon **in Arabic
script** (هيت سماش، باور كراش، ميكس أب). Latin words inside an Arabic paragraph render fine, but a
cell or line that starts with one is laid out left-to-right (see "Right-to-left languages" in
SKILL.md), and script keeps the prose readable aloud.

Keep in Latin only notation and abbreviations: `SNK`, `KND`, `CH`, `i10`, `WS`, `FC`, stance
abbreviations.

## Register

Modern Standard Arabic as in the gaming press. Address the reader in the generic second person
singular (يمكنك، اضغط) as [Saudi Gamer] does. Western digits (notation uses them too). Arabic comma
، and question mark ؟; full stop is `.`.

First-person verbs are not gendered in Arabic, but adjectives and past participles about the author
are: use neutral phrasing for authors of unknown gender (see SKILL.md).

## Research note (2026-11-03)

No Arabic player-written Tekken guide or glossary was found. Terms marked "No source" were chosen
for consistency and need a check by an Arabic-speaking player, especially: launcher (لانشر), safe /
unsafe, sidestep (سايد ستيب), backturn, high / low crush, while standing, string (سلسلة), turtle,
and the Heat / Rage transliterations. The game ships an Arabic interface; its official wording was
not available to check.

text and stay verbatim: put the Arabic in brackets right after them. Arabic-only emphasis uses «».
