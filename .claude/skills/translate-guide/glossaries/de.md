# German Tekken glossary

Terminology for translating tekkendocs character guides into German. Taken from German Tekken and
fighting-game sources, not invented. **Append to this file** whenever a run needs a term that is not
here, and record where it came from.

## Approach

German players and German guides keep almost all English fighting-game jargon (Mid, Launcher,
Punish, Counter Hit, Sidestep) and use it with German grammar: nouns are capitalized and take a
German article, verbs get German endings ("punishen", "gelauncht", "sidesteppen", "blocken"). Only
plain words are translated (Wand, Wurf, Schaden, Reichweite, Hocke). The official German Tekken 8
texts keep the system names in English (Heat, Heat Smash, Rage Art). Move and stance names stay in
English (Deathfist, Demolition Man, Deep Dive, Sway).

## Character names

Unchanged, with German genitive -s: Pauls, Lidias, Steves, Clives, Annas, Dragunovs. Mishimas.

## Register

Informal "du", like most German gaming guides (Red Bull, community guides). Keep the source's
casual tone ("teilt ordentlich aus", "Party angesagt").

## Kunimitsu

Keep Setsunagake (SET), Katon (KAT), Backturn, Hopkick, Option Select, Snake Edge, EWGF, Flash in
English. "duckable" → "lässt sich ducken", "challenge SET" → "SET challengen".

## Dragunov

Keep Rushdown, Turtle, Tackle (verb "tacklen"), Sabaki, Pushback, Hitbox, Crouch Cancel, Sweep,
Mind Games, Clean Hit, Gameplan, Read in English, as German Tekken players do (no German Dragunov
guide was found; the general rule above applies). "jailing string" → "jailender String";
"natural on hit" → "auf Hit natural"; "crouch throw" → "Wurf gegen den hockenden Gegner";
"unbreakable" → "unbreakbar"; "walk (a move)" → "sidewalken"; "Jab float" → "mit einem Jab floaten".
Sneak (SNK), Pigeon Roll (PGN), Combat Sambo, backswing blow stay in English.

## Fahkumram

Garuda Force (GRF) and Rama (RAM) stay in English, like other stance names: "Garuda-Force-Install",
"Rama-Stance", "Rama-Mixup". "install" → der Install; "unblockable" → unblockbar; "can not be
parried" → kann nicht geparryt werden; "hits grounded" → trifft am Boden liegende Gegner; "jails" →
jailt; "knowledge check" → Knowledge Check. (No German Fahkumram guide found; general rule above.)

## Clive
