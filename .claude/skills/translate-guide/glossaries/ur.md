# Urdu Tekken glossary

Terminology for translating tekkendocs character guides into Urdu. **Append to this file** whenever
a run needs a term that is not here, and record where it came from.

## Approach

There is essentially no written Urdu Tekken material: searches for Urdu guides turn up only news
coverage of Pakistani players. Pakistani players talk about the game in English terms mixed into
Urdu or Punjabi. Urdu news writes gaming words as **English loanwords in Urdu script** (ٹیکن,
ویڈیو گیم, گیمرز). Follow that convention: transliterate the English Tekken term into Urdu script
rather than coining a native Urdu word. It also keeps prose free of Latin runs, which disrupt
right-to-left text (see "Right-to-left languages" in SKILL.md).

Keep in Latin only notation and abbreviations: `SNK`, `PGN`, `KND`, `SSL`, `i10`, `CH`.

## Register

Polite آپ form, plain modern Urdu as in Urdu news sites. Western digits. Urdu full stop ۔ and
comma ، (also between listed commands).
