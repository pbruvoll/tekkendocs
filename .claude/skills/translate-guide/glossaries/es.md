# Spanish Tekken glossary

Terminology for translating tekkendocs character guides into Spanish (neutral / Latin American,
`es`). Taken from Spanish-language Tekken sources, not invented. **Append to this file** whenever a
run needs a term that is not here, and record where it came from.

## Approach

Spanish-speaking Tekken players and the Spanish press keep most competitive jargon in English
(launcher, poke, juggle, counter hit, Heat Smash, frame data) and use Spanish for the general
vocabulary around it. Follow that: an over-translated term ("lanzador", "aporreo") reads as
unnatural to the audience. Use neutral Latin American vocabulary (tú, "agarre", "tacleada"), avoid
Spain-only forms (vosotros, "placaje").

## Kept in English

launcher, poke, jab, string, juggle, combo, counter hit (CH), frame data, hitbox, hurtbox, backdash,
crouch dash, sidestep, tracking, homing, wall splat, knockdown (KND), clean hit, mixup, 50/50,
frame trap, whiff, rushdown, chip, sabaki, parry, reversal, Power Crush, While Standing, Pigeon Roll,
Sneak (SNK), and all Heat / Rage names (Heat, Heat Engager, Heat Smash, Heat Burst, Heat Dash, Rage,
Rage Art).

## Character names

Same as English (Dragunov, Hwoarang, Bryan, Zafina, Lili, Kuma/Panda).

## Paul

Keep the move and stance names in English: Deathfist, Demolition Man (demoman), Hammer of the Gods,
shredder, hatchet kick, sway / backsway, backsway cancel, Deep Dive, guard break, fuzzy duck, tech
roll, oki, frame tight, keep-out. Translated: "range 2/3" → rango 2/3, "litmus test" → prueba de
fuego, side-switching throws → agarres que cambian de lado.

## Register

Informal tú, direct coaching voice ("Toma tu turno", "no lo machaques").

## Kunimitsu

Keep Setsunagake (SET), Katon (KAT), hopkick, option select, snake edge, EWGF in English.
Translated: backturn → de espaldas ("queda de espaldas", "mixup de espaldas"), duckable → se puede
agachar, full crouch mixup → mixup desde agachado.
Avoid English verbs used bare: "hacerle parry", "hacerle low parry", "desgastar (chip)", "evitar caminando" (not "caminar una opción").

## Fahkumram

Garuda Force (GRF) and Rama (RAM) stay in English: "install Garuda Force", "postura Rama", "mixup
de Rama". Translated: unblockable → imbloqueable, hits grounded → golpea al rival en el suelo,
can not be parried → no se le puede hacer parry, combos naturally → combea. Kept: install, guard
break, knowledge check.

## Xiaoyu

AOP (Art of Phoenix) and Hypnotist (HYP) stay in English; backturn → de espaldas, as for Kunimitsu
("parry de espaldas", "50/50 de espaldas"). Cali Roll → rodada (Cali Roll); Hop and Throw, hell sweep,
Flash (Yoshimitsu) stay English. high crush → crushear ("crushea altos", as in earlier guides);
tip range → a punta de alcance; twitch confirm → confirmar según la reacción del rival; steps (HYP) →
pasos; Bear → oso.

## Alisa

Destructive Form (DES), Boot (SBT), Dual Boot (DBT), Backup (BKP) stay in English ("posturas Boot").
chainsaws → motosierras; Hellsweep, Hard Reset, Evasive Action, Demon Paw stay English; spike
knockdown → knockdown de clavado; airborne → deja a Alisa en el aire; hail mary → launcher a la
desesperada; goated → ser un crack; mental frames → «frames mentales» (never ASCII quotes in prose:
they become command links).

## Hwoarang

Stance names stay in English: Left Flamingo (LFS), Right Flamingo (RFS), Right Foot Forward (RFF),
"postura Flamingo"; JFSR, hellsweep kept. wall carry → llevar a la pared; balcony → balcón;
whiff punisher → castigo de whiff.

## Clive

ZAN is a gauge, not a stance (frame data: "Increases ZAN gauge"): "gives ZAN" → "da ZAN", never "ir a ZAN".

Phoenix Shift (PHX) stays English ("postura PHX"); rekka → el rekka; ZAN, GAR, blue spark kept.
Torgal → "los ataques del perro (Torgal)"; poser → para lucirse; "damn it" → carajo (LatAm).

## Leo

Leo's gender is not stated: repeat "Leo" instead of él/ella.
