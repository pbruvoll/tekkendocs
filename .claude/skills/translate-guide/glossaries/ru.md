# Russian Tekken glossary

Terminology for translating tekkendocs character guides into Russian. Taken from Russian Tekken and
fighting-game sources, not invented. **Append to this file** whenever a run needs a term that is not
here, and record where it came from.

## Approach

Russian-speaking players transliterate most English fighting-game jargon into Cyrillic (мид, паниш,
лаунчер) and the Russian guides do the same, so follow that. When sources spell a term differently, prefer the
Tekken 8 specific guide on Steam (the main source below) over general fighting-game glossaries.
The official Russian localization of Tekken 8 provides the system names (Хит, Ярость). Move and stance
names are left in English, in Latin script (Deathfist, Demolition Man, sway, Deep Dive), as Russian
guides do.

## Register

Formal "вы", like a written guide. Keep the source's casual tone otherwise ("жмёт кнопки",
"бьёт очень больно").
