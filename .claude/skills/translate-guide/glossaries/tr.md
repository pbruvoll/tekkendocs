# Turkish Tekken glossary

Terminology for translating tekkendocs character guides into Turkish. Taken from Turkish Tekken and
gaming sources, not invented. **Append to this file** whenever a run needs a term that is not here,
and record where it came from.

## Approach

The Turkish gaming press and Turkish players keep the fighting-game jargon and all system names in
English (Heat, Heat Smash, Rage, round, chip damage, replay, Ranked) and only turn "combo" into
**kombo**. Plain words are Turkish (duvar, hasar, menzil, hareket, rakip, blok). Fully translated
jargon, as in the machine-translated Steam guide below ("fırlatıcı" for launcher, "hokkabazlık" for
juggle), reads as wrong to Turkish players: do not use it.

English loanwords take Turkish suffixes after an apostrophe, with vowel harmony following the
**pronunciation**: launcher'ı, punisher'lar, stance'e, string'i, mixup'lar, Heat'e, Heat Smash'i,
low'u, mid'i, high'ı, sidestep'i, combo → kombo'yu is written **komboyu** (kombo is a Turkish word
now, no apostrophe). Character names and notation also take an apostrophe: Paul'un, Lidia'nın,
Kunimitsu'nun, Dragunov'un, Hwoarang'ın, Xiaoyu'nun, Alisa'nın, Fahkumram'ın; "df+1"'i is avoided by
rephrasing ("df+1 hareketi") since a quoted command must stay intact.

## Register

Informal "sen" (öğren, kullan), as Turkish gaming sites write for players. Keep the source's casual
tone.

## Quoted commands and suffixes

A suffix after a quoted command needs an apostrophe outside the quotes, `"B+4,3"'e`, never inside
(the quoted text must stay byte-identical). Without it the suffix renders glued to the command link.
Quoted English words that the source uses as bold labels or links stay English and get a Turkish
gloss: `"clunky" (hantal)`, `"Polarising Full Crouch and Backturn Mix:" (tam çömelme ve sırtı dönük
keskin mixup)`, `"electric."`, `"main" (ana)`.

## Characters

- **Backturn / BT** (Kunimitsu, Xiaoyu): sırtı dönük ("sırtı dönük mixup", "sırtı dönük pozisyona
  geçer"); keep BT in notation and as a short noun ("BT'ye geçer").
- **Stance names** stay English like the system names: Setsunagake (SET), Katon (KAT), Sneak (SNK),
  Pigeon Roll (PGN), Garuda Force (GRF), Rama, Art of Phoenix (AOP), Hypnotist (HYP), Destructive
  Form (DES), Boot (SBT), Dual Boot (DBT), Backup (BKP), Left/Right Flamingo (LFS/RFS), Right Foot
  Forward (RFF).
- **Move names** stay English: Deathfist, Demolition Man, Hammer of the Gods, Deep Dive, backsway,
  Shredder, hatchet kick, Hellsweep, Cali Roll, Hop and Throw, Demon Paw, Hard Reset, JFSR.
- turtle (player) → kaplumbağa gibi kapanan / kaplumbağa oynayan rakip; litmus test → turnusol
  testi; jail → jail'lemek; natural combo → natural kombo; hits grounded → yerdeki rakibe vurur;
  unblockable → bloklanamaz; knowledge check → knowledge check (bölüm adı); hail mary → çaresizlikten
  atılan; goated → yeterince iyiysen; lab out → training modunda bul; Bear → ayı.

## Clive

ZAN is a gauge, not a stance (frame data: "Increases ZAN gauge"): "gives ZAN" → "ZAN verir / ZAN göstergesini doldurur", never "ZAN'a geçmek".

Phoenix Shift (PHX) stays English ("PHX stance'i"); rekka → rekka ("rekka'yı"); ZAN, GAR, blue
spark kept. Torgal → "köpeğin (Torgal) saldırıları"; poser → şov için.

## Kazuya

wavedash, vortex, hellsweep, EWGF, electric, Stature Kick, Devil stomp kept ("wavedash'ten",
"vortex başlatıcısı"). pancake flip → pankek gibi çevirmek; meaty → meaty; stagger low → stagger
low; down jab → çömelerek atılan jab.

## Leo

Jin Ji Du Li (KNK), Fo Bu (BOK, "Bok stance'i"), Lightning Glare (LTG, install) stay English;
hopkick, sweep bang, slash kick, Steel Pedal kept. Never ASCII quotes around a glossed word
(‘safe’, not "safe"): they become command links.

## Miary Zo

Morengy Miroso (MOR), Baobab Mihira (BAO), Tromba, Fighting God, ogre horn flip, demon paw kept.
silver bullet → gümüş kurşun; situation confirm → durum confirm'ü.

## Jack-8

Gamma Howl (GMH) and Gamma Charge (GMC) stay English ("Gamma Howl stance'i"). red bubble → kırmızı baloncuklu. For "raw" (unprepared) use "hazırlıksız", not "çiğ çiğ" (an eating idiom).

## Shaheen

Stealth Step (SNK), Al Ghul Master, AK WS3 loop'ları stay English. slide → kayma; orbital → orbital (rakibi havada döndüren launcher).

## Armor King

## Law

## Leroy

Hermit (HRM, "Hermit stance'i"), Wing Chun, G-Clef, perfect parry, Stature Kick stay English. cane → baston. Glossed phrases use ‘…’ (‘al ve oyna’), never ASCII quotes.

## Heihachi

## Nina

## Jin

## Lars

## Raven

## Reina
